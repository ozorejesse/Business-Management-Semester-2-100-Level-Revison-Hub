Functions & Transformations

Vertical Shift: \( y = f(x) + c \) (up), \( y = f(x) - c \) (down)

Horizontal Shift: \( y = f(x - c) \) (right), \( y = f(x + c) \) (left)

Reflection: \( y = -f(x) \) (x-axis), \( y = f(-x) \) (y-axis)

Limits & Continuity

Limit of sin(x)/x: \( \lim_{x\to0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1 \)

IVT: If f is continuous on [a,b] and k is between f(a) and f(b), then there exists c in [a,b] such that f(c)=k.

Differentiation

Power Rule: \( \frac{d}{dx}(x^n) = nx^{n-1} \)

Product Rule: \( (uv)' = u'v + uv' \)

Quotient Rule: \( (\frac{u}{v})' = \frac{u'v - uv'}{v^2} \)

Chain Rule: \( \frac{d}{dx}f(g(x)) = f'(g(x))g'(x) \)

Higher Derivatives & Applications

Second Derivative Test: If \( f'(c)=0 \) and \( f''(c) > 0 \), local minimum. If \( f''(c) < 0 \), local maximum.

Marginal Cost: \( C'(x) \) is the cost of producing one additional unit.

Related Rates: \( \frac{dV}{dt} = \frac{dV}{dx} \times \frac{dx}{dt} \)

Integration

Indefinite Integral: \( \int x^n dx = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} + C \)

Definite Integral: \( \int_a^b f(x) dx = F(b) - F(a) \)

Area Under Curve: \( A = \int_a^b y \, dx \)

Volume of Revolution: \( V = \int_a^b \pi y^2 \, dx \)

Kinematics: \( v = \int a \, dt \), \( s = \int v \, dt \)
