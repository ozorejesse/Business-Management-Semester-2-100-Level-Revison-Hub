Permutations & Combinations

Permutations: \( ^nP_r = \frac{n!}{(n-r)!} \)

Circular Permutations: \( (n-1)! \)

Combinations: \( ^nC_r = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!} \)

Pascal's Rule: \( ^nC_r + ^nC_{r-1} = ^{n+1}C_r \)

Binomial Theorem

Expansion: \( (x+y)^n = \sum_{r=0}^{n} ^nC_r x^{n-r}y^r \)

General Term: \( T_{r+1} = ^nC_r x^{n-r}y^r \)

For negative/fractional index: \( (1+x)^n = 1 + nx + \frac{n(n-1)}{2!}x^2 + \dots \)

Sequences & Series

AP nth term: \( a_n = a + (n-1)d \)

AP sum: \( S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n-1)d] \)

GP nth term: \( a_n = ar^{n-1} \)

GP sum: \( S_n = \frac{a(1-r^n)}{1-r} \)

GP sum to infinity: \( S_\infty = \frac{a}{1-r} \) (for \( |r|<1 \))

Complex Numbers

Rectangular: \( z = a + bi \)

Polar: \( z = r(\cos \theta + i\sin \theta) \)

Exponential: \( z = re^{i\theta} \)

Magnitude: \( r = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} \), Argument: \( \theta = \tan^{-1}(\frac{b}{a}) \)

De Moivre: \( z^n = r^n(\cos n\theta + i\sin n\theta) \)

Trigonometry

Pythagorean: \( \sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1 \), \( 1 + \tan^2\theta = \sec^2\theta \), \( 1 + \cot^2\theta = \csc^2\theta \)

Special Angles: \( \sin 30^\circ = \frac{1}{2} \), \( \cos 30^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \), \( \tan 45^\circ = 1 \), \( \sin 60^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \)

Sets & Venn Diagrams

Intersection: \( A \cap B \)

Union: \( A \cup B \)

Complement: \( A' \) or \( A^c \)

De Morgan's Laws: \( (A \cup B)' = A' \cap B' \), \( (A \cap B)' = A' \cup B' \)
